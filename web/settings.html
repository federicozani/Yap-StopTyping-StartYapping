Your privacy

Yap records your microphone only while you hold the shortcut. That audio (and, for the Rewrite button, the text you have selected) is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to the provider you choose — Google Gemini or OpenAI — which turns it into finished text that Yap pastes back. Nothing else leaves your Mac: no account, no analytics, no tracking.

You bring your own API key. It is stored only in ~/.yap/config.json on this Mac and is sent solely to your chosen provider. Your transcripts are never written to Yap’s log.

Audio and text you send are processed by that provider under their terms — see Google’s and OpenAI’s privacy policies for how they handle API data.
